Our Story & Mandate

Founded in 2010, The Harvester began as a dedicated group of believers meeting with a shared passion for seeing lives transformed and hearts established in the power of Jesus Christ.

Over the years, God has expanded our fellowship into a vibrant multicultural family. Our heart remains steadfast: to foster true worship, build authentic community, and raise disciples equipped to make lasting kingdom impact.

Today, we continue to serve the Rainham community and beyond, striving to be a beacon of hope, restoration, and spiritual growth for every generation.
