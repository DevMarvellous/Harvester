Our Story

Founded in 2010, The Harvester began as a small group of believers meeting in a living room with a shared passion for seeing lives transformed by the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Over the years, we have grown into a diverse and vibrant community, but our core mission remains the same: to love God, love people, and make disciples.

Today, we continue to serve the Rainham community and beyond, striving to be a beacon of hope and a place where everyone can find a sense of belonging.
