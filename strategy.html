About Us

The Harvester is a community for all nations. We are a ministry with passion to see everyone make heaven.

At The Harvester, we are committed to help each and every one discover their real purpose in life, and in discovering your gifts to maximise your potentials, and fulfilling destinies, in turn impacting our surroundings positively.

Our Vision

To see the manifestation of the power of God raising the generations of true worshippers.

As a purpose driven community, we share the good news of Jesus Christ with clarity for all to see the light of life, and be supernaturally established in the will of God.

Our Mandates

  • Be ready in all seasons (Lk 8:16-18)
  • Be fruitful (Jn 15:16-17)
  • Be a true servant (2 Tim 2:15)
  • Be worshippers with Spiritual insight (Rom 8:6-8)

THE HARVESTER

A place where faith grows, lives are transformed, and Christ is glorified.
