Strategic Church Model

A creative expression of The Harvester Ministry’s identity, call, vision, and mandate.

1. Identity & Core Mission

Who are we? We are a family value-driven organization founded on honesty, humility, and excellence in offering service to God and fellow humans.

  • Core Aim: To ENGAGE and EMPOWER individuals met, or whom God chooses to bring to the ministry, from diverse backgrounds.
  • Primary Goal: To awaken and establish generations of true worshippers of God on earth.
  • Core Belief: God has created us all for His pleasure in worshipping Him.

2. The Call & RCCG Mandate

The Call Theme: "Not to be divided; but to be strengthened." This ministry emerged from Sanctuary of Power under the Great Commission of the Redeemed Christian Church of God.

RCCG’s Five-Fold Mandate

  1. To Make Heaven.
  2. To take as many people as possible with us.
  3. To have a member of RCCG in every family of all nations.
  4. To live a life of holiness.
  5. To accomplish numbers 2 & 3 above through church planting:
  • Within 5 minutes walking distance in every city and town of developing countries.
  • Within 5 minutes driving distance in every city of developed countries.

The Harvester Ministry is uniquely called to raise true worshippers and strengthen the body of Christ through obedience to this divine mandate.

3. Vision & Passion

Vision: To raise generations of true worshippers who serve God in Spirit and in truth.

Scriptural Anchor: Luke 10:2 – "The harvest truly is great, but the laborers are few; therefore pray the Lord of the harvest to send out laborers into His harvest."

Passion: To fully realize the divine purpose of creation in His Son, the Second Covenant.

Ultimate Objective: To operate as a passion-driven church, divinely connected and graced to strengthen the body of Christ so that the plan of heaven is accomplished: "We must all make Heaven."

4. The Mandate

Our walk with God is shaped by clarity and obedience in every season.

  1. To be Ready in all seasons (Luke 8:16-18)
  2. To be Fruitful (John 15:16-17)
  3. To be Worshippers with Spiritual insight (Romans 8:6-8)
  4. To be a True Servant in our services (2 Timothy 2:15)

5. Core Values

Grounded in 1 Corinthians 13:1-4, our culture is shaped by love and excellence.

Humility

To do all in simplicity by reverencing God.

Honesty

To be sincere in our services so we remain relatable to all.

Excellence

To carry out every task in a timely, orderly, and excellent manner.

Love

The overriding principle: "If you do all but do not LOVE; then you gain nothing."

THE HARVESTER

A place where faith grows, lives are transformed, and Christ is glorified.
