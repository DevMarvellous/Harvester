A Place You Can Call Home

At The Harvester, we believe that church is more than just a Sunday morning gathering — it is a supportive family where everyone is embraced, empowered, and equipped to fulfil their God-given destiny.

  • Warm Multicultural Welcome: People from all walks of life united in Christ.
  • Engaging Word & Worship: Practical biblical teaching that impacts daily living.
